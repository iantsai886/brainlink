BrainLink

One Obsidian / Markdown second brain for every AI you use: Claude, ChatGPT Codex, Cursor, Gemini, and more.

This installs:

  • BrainLink.app in Applications (opens Setup)
  • the brainlink command in /usr/local/bin

Your notes never leave this Mac. BrainLink only changes an AI app's settings when you press Connect, and keeps a backup each time.
