BrainLink is installed

Setup opens in your browser. If it doesn't, open BrainLink from Applications.

  1. Choose your vault folder (or try the sample vault).
  2. Press Connect for each AI app.
  3. In that AI, say "Connect to" followed by your agent's name.

Uninstall: drag BrainLink to the Trash and run sudo rm /usr/local/bin/brainlink. Your notes and AI apps keep working; use Disconnect in Setup first to remove BrainLink from them.
