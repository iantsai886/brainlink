The problem

Each AI starts from zero, and the ones that can write make a mess

You paste the same context into Claude in the morning and into ChatGPT in the afternoon. Decisions made in one chat never reach the next one. When an AI can edit your notes, it rewrites them without a trace. BrainLink gives every AI the same memory and the same rules.

You, in Claude"Connect to Chief of Staff"The AI loads the agent's role, current goal, recent decisions and open handoffs from your vault, and flags notes that are out of date.
You, in Cursor or Codex, later"What did we decide about the launch?"A different AI finds the same decision by searching the same vault, and cites the note it came from.
You, at the end"Chief of Staff close loop"The session's decisions and next actions are appended to the agent's log, signed by the AI that wrote them.

Safe by design

Your rules are enforced in software, not left to a prompt

Nothing is overwrittenAIs can only add new notes or append to existing ones. Nothing is ever deleted.
Preview before writeEvery write shows a preview first. Your close phrase approves the capture.
Signed editsEach note records which AI, agent and session wrote it, with a timestamp.
Secrets refusedAPI keys, tokens, card and account numbers are blocked from entering the vault.
Private stays privateLimit each AI to specific vaults, make a connection read-only, or set a journal to proposals only.
Stays on your computerBrainLink runs locally. Your notes are never sent to Iango.
Smart by default

A brain that organises itself as your AIs use it

You don't need Obsidian or a filing system. BrainLink does the linking and the bookkeeping.

Notes link themselvesEvery new note links the people, projects and notes it mentions, plus the most related ones. A Home note links everything.
Finds what you meantKeyword search with filters by folder, tag and date. Add a free local model and it also searches by meaning.
"Brief me"What changed this week, open tasks, next actions and decisions waiting on you, in one answer.
Remembers youSay "remember that I prefer short answers" once; every connected AI knows it from then on.
Meeting notes from transcriptsPaste a transcript and get a meeting note with attendees linked and action items as tasks.
One command, every AISave a prompt like "Weekly review" once; it appears as a command in Claude, Cursor and every other connected app.
