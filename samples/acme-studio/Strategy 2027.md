# Strategy 2027

## Positioning

Acme designs brand systems and fast marketing websites for independent hospitality and health businesses. We win on speed: a brand sprint in two weeks and a launch-ready site in four.

## Goals

1. Grow recurring care plans from 6 to 15 clients.
2. Keep project gross margin above 55%.
3. Productise the brand sprint as a fixed-price offer.

## Bets

- Use AI drafting for first-round copy and moodboards; keep humans on final design decisions.
- Publish one case study per month.
