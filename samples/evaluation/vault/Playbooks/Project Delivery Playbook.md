# Project Delivery Playbook

## Kickoff

Confirm goals, decision makers and the one metric the site must move. Write it in the project note.

## Weekly rhythm

Monday plan, Wednesday client check-in, Friday demo. Every decision goes in the project note with a date.

## Quality gates

- Accessibility check before launch.
- Page speed: Largest Contentful Paint under 2.5 seconds on mobile.
- Client sign-off in writing.

## Retrospective

Within a week of launch, capture what to repeat and what to change in Knowledge Intake.
